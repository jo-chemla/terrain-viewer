Checked 2026-11-25 (live GPU renderer and shadow:// tiles): azimuth is the compass bearing of the sun (toward it, clockwise from north), step (east, south) = (sin az, −cos az); altitude in degrees, radians once; metres per texel = equator / (tileSize · 2^z) · cos(lat); heights in metres. No bug.

Why users see shadows only under very low suns: the light pad (`SphericalXYPad`, `LightControlOverlay`) maps its radius to cos(altitude), so 30° and below is the outer 13 % of the radius; and a DTM (Mapterhorn) has few slopes steeper than 30° — a slope casts only when steeper than the sun is high. A DSM adds buildings/trees. Hence the toggle "Terrain (+ buildings if DSM)".

**Why:** a future report of "shadows only at the horizon" should not send anyone hunting a units bug.

**How to apply:** the live shader is generated by `shadowFragmentShader()` in `lib/shadow-live-math.ts`, next to `sunVisibilityCpu()` (its line-by-line twin). Change both together; `lib/shadow-live-math.test.ts` (synthetic cliff, curvature, far field, soft edge, tile kernel on the same cliff) and `node scripts/bench-shadow.mjs --live-only` (runs the shader string in Chrome, checks 100 % same pixels as the twin) catch drift.
