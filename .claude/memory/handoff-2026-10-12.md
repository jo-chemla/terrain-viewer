  pad is held the panel fades except the held unit, with a band behind it;
  the SOURCES/OPTIONS/DETECTORS/TOOLS rows fade too.
- **Phone portrait sheet** ([[mobile-layout-frames]]): every side margin and
  vertical gap is 8 px (edge controls, timeline, docked profile, minimap,
  clock toggle, scale bar); phone landscape keeps the timeline's pills on one
  scrollable line (`sm-tall` variant). Sliders use Base UI's
  `thumbAlignment="center"` after the thumbs vanished on a Pixel 10 Pro
  (Chrome Android) in edge mode; Jonathan still has to confirm on the phone.
- **Sources**: Library before Add Terrain / Add Basemap; a library row's add
  selects the terrain; the source info dialog shows Raster size (W × H px)
  and CRS (titiler `/cog/info` or geotiff.js), a red "file not readable" pill
  with the reason when the file is gone (a Planet COG whose URL answers 404:
  the record mixed a Philippines SkySat item with a Venezuela path, Planet's
  catalog has been rebuilt since; re-add it from the Philippines release).
- **Building shadows** ([[building-shadows-tiles]]): footprints from the
  layer's own z14 OpenFreeMap tiles around the viewport (cap 16 tiles), not
  `querySourceFeatures`; the straight edge in the docs' 17:00 pictures is a
  real building's shadow under a sun at azimuth 271°.
- **Library** ([[titiler-dem-gotchas]]): Kilauea 2018 lower East Rift Zone
  VRT and its lava difference back with `minzoom: 13` (titiler serves the
  untiled mosaic from z13; the picker's fit lands at a source's minzoom);
  its terraces at fissure 8 are in the file (holes filled by the gridding).
  The two La Palma DSMs and their difference removed: plain GeoTIFFs, no
  overviews, not COGs; all six Tajogaite datasets on OpenTopography are the
  same format, no COG exists.
- **Panel**: a GENERAL fold row above the top sections.
- **Desktop**: `launcher.exe "<project.zip>"` imports on the installed light
