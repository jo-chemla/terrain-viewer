# Land cover, canopy height, GlobalBuildingAtlas (surveyed 2026-11-21, second pass the same day)

**Land cover overlays added** (SAMPLE_BASEMAPS_SOURCES, all CORS-open, no key, each fetched with curl and drawn in headless Chrome): ESA WorldCover 2020 (Terrascope mapproxy, same service as 2021), Esri / Impact Observatory 10 m (`ic.imagery1.arcgis.com/.../Sentinel2_10m_LandCover/ImageServer/exportImage`, type `wms` with `{bbox-epsg-3857}`), MODIS IGBP 500 m (NASA GIBS), Corine 2018 (EEA discomap `Corine/CLC2018_WM/MapServer/export`; the service has no tile cache, `/tile/` is 404), NLCD 2021 (MRLC GeoServer WMS), IGN OCS GE couverture and usage 2021-2023 and CoSIA 2021-2023 (data.geopf.fr WMTS). Second pass: Copernicus LCFM LCM-10 2020 (10 m), Copernicus LC100 2019 and 2015 (100 m), below.

## Copernicus Global Land Cover 100 m (CGLS-LC100 v3) and LCFM LCM-10

- **2019, added** (`copernicus-cgls-lc100-2019`): `https://data.source.coop/cboettig/land-cover/cgls-lc100-2019-cog.tif`, the Boettiger Lab's COG of the Zenodo file (2.4 GB, EPSG:4326, uint8 with the colour table, 512 px blocks, 10 overviews, nodata 255, CC BY 4.0). CORS `*`, Range 206, preflight allows `range`. Same file at `https://s3-west.nrp-nautilus.io/public-land-cover/cgls-lc100-2019-cog.tif`. Type `cog`, `cogViaTitiler` (geographic file), `categorical`. titiler.xyz: about 1 s a tile at any zoom from 0.
- **2015, added** (`copernicus-cgls-lc100-2015`): the publisher's file on Zenodo (`zenodo.org/records/3939038/files/PROBAV_LC100_global_v3.0.1_2015-base_Discrete-Classification-map_EPSG-4326.tif`). Zenodo answers Range (206) on both `/records/<id>/files/<name>` and `/api/records/<id>/files/<name>/content`, CORS `*` on the api form only, 133 requests a minute. The file is tiled (256 px, deflate) but has **no overviews**: titiler.xyz answers z6 and up in 4 to 10 s, 500 at z3. 24 parallel z8 tiles all came back 200 in about 10 s. Hence minzoom 6 and "slow" in the name. 2016, 2017, 2018: records 3518026, 3518036, 3518038, same form, not added.
- **`categorical: true`** (CustomBasemapSource, `buildRasterTileSource`): asks titiler for `resampling=nearest&reproject=nearest`. With the default bilinear the class codes are blended and the tile has holes along every class edge (seen on a z10 tile over Lyon). Library and project files only; the source form keeps the flag but has no switch.
- **LCFM LCM-10 2020, added** (`copernicus-lcfm-lcm10-2020`): the `LCM_2020` layer of the Terrascope mapproxy is the Copernicus Land Monitoring Service's 10 m map (Terrascope news of 2026-05-26, STAC collection `stac.terrascope.be/collections/lcfm-lcm-10`, CC BY 4.0). Tiles 200 with CORS `*` from z1 to z17 (z0 is 400).
- Dead ends: `services.terrascope.be/wms/v2` and `/wmts/v2` still do not answer (connection failure, third and fourth try); the bucket `vito.landcover.global` does not exist (NoSuchBucket on eu-central-1 and eu-west-1); `land.copernicus.eu/arcgis` is 404; the discomap ImageServer has no global land cover folder. The official COGs are on the Copernicus Data Space (`stac.dataspace.copernicus.eu/v1/collections/clms_lc_global_100m_yearly_v3_cog` v3.1.2, and `clms_lcm_global_10m_yearly_v1_cog`) but the assets are `s3://eodata/...` with an https alternate that needs an OIDC login. The Terrascope STAC's LCM-10 COGs point at `services.terrascope.be/download/...`, the host that does not answer.
- Also on `cboettig/land-cover` (not added, leads): Annual NLCD 1985-2025 COGs (`nlcd/`), ETH Global Canopy Height 2020 10 m as a VRT over 2 651 COGs (`canopy-height-2020/height.vrt`, CC BY 4.0), a candidate terrain source.

## Dynamic World: no public copy, nothing added

Earth Engine only, confirmed. `dynamicworld.app/explore` is an iframe of the Earth Engine App `earthoutreach.users.earthengine.app/view/dynamicworldapp`: its tiles are Earth Engine map ids made per session with an OAuth token the page embeds and that expires in one hour (`authTokenExpiresInMs: 3599000`), so there is no stable tile address. No bucket: `dynamicworld-public`, `gcp-public-data-dynamicworld` are 404, `dynamic-world` and `dynamicworld` are 401 (private), `earthengine-public` holds only landsat, srtm, ned, s1, egms samples. Planetary Computer has no Dynamic World collection (its `io-lulc-annual-v02` is the Esri / Impact Observatory map already in the library). Nothing in the AWS Open Data registry, nothing among the 739 products of Source Cooperative (listed 2026-11-21), Hugging Face has only the training labels (`fatmur/dynamicworld`). The GEM-15 trick does not apply: GEM-15 is files on a public bucket, Dynamic World is an Earth Engine collection with no exported copy. The only route is an Earth Engine project of one's own (an export to COG, or a small tile proxy with a service account).

## GlobalBuildingAtlas (Zhu et al. 2025, ESSD 17, 6647): nothing added

**Heights (GBA.Height, 3 m, CC BY-NC 4.0): reachable over HTTPS, not streamable.** mediaTUM's share is a Nextcloud: `https://dataserv.ub.tum.de/public.php/dav/files/m1782307/Height/<continent>/<tile>.zip` answers GET and Range (206) with no login; a PROPFIND listing and a HEAD need the header `X-Requested-With: XMLHttpRequest` (401 without), no CORS header, preflight 401. Each 5 degree tile is one zip of 0.1 to 105 GB holding 0.2 degree GeoTIFFs named `<W>_<N>_<E>_<S>_sr_ss.tif`: Float32, UTM, nodata -1, **one-row strips (Block 4407x1), no overviews, and deflated inside the zip** (method 8), so a window cannot be read without inflating the member from its start. Measured with GDAL (`/vsizip//vsicurl/`, the header set through `GDAL_HTTP_HEADERS`): 3.5 s for the header, 55 s for one 256 px window of a 146 MB member. titiler.xyz cannot open it at all (its HEAD gets 401). Making it streamable means downloading 36 TB and rewriting it as COGs.

**Polygons with heights: readable, vector only.**
- `https://data.source.coop/tge-labs/globalbuildingatlas-lod1/<tile>.parquet` (Taylor Geospatial): 922 GeoParquet files, one per 5 degree tile (`e000_n10_e005_n05`), 210 GB, up to 2.3 GB a file. Columns `source` (`google`, `ms`, `ours2`, `osm` seen in one tile; the paper also lists 3D-GloBFP), `id`, `height` (metres, -999 no data), `var` (height variance), `region`, `bbox` (xmin, ymin, xmax, ymax), `geometry` (WKB polygon, EPSG:4326). CORS `*`, Range 206.
- `https://data.source.coop/walkthru-earth/indices/building/globalbuildingatlas/zoomlevel=<2..14>/data*.parquet` (walkthru.earth): the same buildings as a pyramid, each building in the zoom level where it first appears, rows sorted by a zoom 14 `quadkey` column, row groups of 131 072 rows; columns `quadkey, source, id, height, var, region, geometry`. z2 to z13 are 6 KB to 288 MB a file; z14 is 16 files, up to 98 GB, 1.27 billion rows in one of them. Also `.../building/v2/h3/h3_res=<3..8>/data.parquet`: per H3 cell `building_count`, `avg_height_m`, `max_height_m`, `total_volume_m3`, `coverage_ratio` (632 KB to 1.9 GB).
- Hugging Face: `zhu-xlab/GBA.LoD1` (CC BY-NC 4.0) and `zhu-xlab/GBA.ODbLPolygon` (ODbL), GeoJSON of 1 to 10 GB a tile, `resolve/main/...` redirects to a CDN with CORS `*` and Range. `cmti/GlobalBuildingAtlas_India_Parquet` holds India without the height column.
- Earth Engine community catalog: `projects/sat-io/open-datasets/GLOBAL_BUILDING_ATLAS/<tile>` feature collections (polygons with a `height` property); the page says the height rasters and LoD1 were not ingested. Earth Engine only.
- PMTiles: only Thailand, `https://gain9999.github.io/thailand-building/GlobalBuildingAtlas/<province>.pmtiles` (77 files, z9-15, layer `buildings`, property `h`), a third party's GitHub Pages.
- Licence: the authors give CC BY-NC 4.0 for the heights and LoD1, ODbL for the polygons; the two Source Cooperative mirrors label their copies CC BY 4.0, which the source licence does not support. Treat heights as CC BY-NC.
- The web viewer (`tubvsig-so2sat-vm1.srv.mwn.de`): its GeoServer is closed to others on purpose. WMS and WFS capabilities 403; the WFS `global3D:lod1_global` needs a `viewerSession` token the page fetches from `/viewer-session`; the GeoWebCache advertises `global3D:lod1_global` as vector tiles but every GetTile answers 400 ("Problem communicating with GeoServer"); `global3D:volume_480m` PNG tiles answer 200 with no CORS header and a 403 preflight. Do not use.
- Stand-in without heights: `vida/google-microsoft-osm-open-buildings` on Source Cooperative has PMTiles per country (`pmtiles/by_country/country_iso=<ISO>/<ISO>.pmtiles`, ODbL, attributes `bf_source`, `confidence`, no height).
- What showing the polygons would take: a PMTiles archive (tippecanoe over the 210 GB of Parquet, a few hundred GB, hosted somewhere with Range and CORS), the `pmtiles` protocol registered, then a `fill-extrusion` layer on `height` as `OsmBuildingsLayer` does with OpenFreeMap's `render_height` in `components/**/VectorBasemapLayer.tsx`. Reading the Parquet pyramid in the browser instead (row groups picked by quadkey with hyparquet, turned into GeoJSON) looks feasible to about zoom 13 (not tried); the zoom 14 files run to 98 GB and thousands of row groups.

**Library search** (`lib/library-search.ts`, tests in `lib/library-search.test.ts`, run with `node --test --experimental-strip-types`): separators ignored, typo fallback only when nothing matches exactly, an optional `keywords` field on entries for other spellings.
