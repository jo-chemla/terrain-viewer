The destinations are the web app Terrain Viewer's "Open in" list (Iconem River-REM, Iconem Search-EO, ESRI Wayback, Google Maps 3D, Google Earth web historical and 3D, Bing Maps 3D, ShadeMap, LiDAR Lens, BBBike MapCompare), plus Terrain Viewer itself, Iconem Historical Satellite, Maxar, and Google Earth Pro on the desktop through a KML file. Derived from NextGIS's Send2GE (send2google_earth) plugin by Maxim Dubinin, GPL v2 or later.
