description=Terrain Viewer's modes (slope, sky view factor, openness, matcap, contours...) as QGIS layers, set from a panel like the app's sidebar.
about=A dock panel with the groups of Terrain Viewer's sidebar. Each checked mode becomes an XYZ layer of a Terrain Viewer tile service (a local one: cd tile-server && pnpm start, or a hosted one), with its settings in the layer's address; changing a setting rewrites the address. The overlays (contours, iso-line, mound candidates, plane slicer, graticule) can also be loaded as vector tiles. "Open from link" loads the layers of a share link. "Freeze" copies the layers drawn now into a group the panel never changes, to compare settings. The panel is built from what the service answers, so new modes and settings appear without a plugin update. Pure Python, no dependency.
