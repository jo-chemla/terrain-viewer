The **Open in …** split button (bottom of the General section; also in the historical timeline footer) opens the current viewport in another web viewer, carrying the camera position over so you land on the same spot. Picking a destination from the dropdown opens it immediately and remembers it as the default for the main button.

## Destinations

- **Iconem [River-REM](/features/river-rem)** — [rem.prod.heritagewatch.ai](https://rem.prod.heritagewatch.ai/) — river-relative elevation models (REM) for the river in view
- **Iconem Search-EO** — [search-eo-imagery.iconem.com](https://search-eo-imagery.iconem.com/) — search commercial/open EO imagery catalogs over the area
- **ESRI Wayback Machine** — [livingatlas.arcgis.com/wayback](https://livingatlas.arcgis.com/wayback/) — opens on the newest Wayback release at this location (the same archive the app's own historical timeline scrubs)
- **Google Maps 3D** — [google.com/maps](https://www.google.com/maps) — Maps' photorealistic 3D globe layer, tilted view
- **Google Earth Historical (web)** — [earth.google.com/web](https://earth.google.com/web/) — top-down with the historical-imagery layer enabled
- **Google Earth 3D (web)** — [earth.google.com/web](https://earth.google.com/web/) — oblique 3D framing, no historical layer
- **Bing Maps 3D** — [bing.com/maps](https://www.bing.com/maps) — Bing's photogrammetry view, with the camera height, heading and tilt carried over
- **ShadeMap (sun and shadows)** — [shademap.app](https://shademap.app/) — terrain, building and tree shadows at the current time, with the bearing and pitch carried over (`/@lat,lng,{zoom}z,{time}t,{bearing}b,{pitch}p,0m`)
- **Mapricot shadows (opens at its own place)** — [mapricot.com/gallery/shadows](https://mapricot.com/gallery/shadows/) — terrain shadows by day of the year and time of day. It reads no location from its URL (neither query nor hash), so it opens at its first preset, Manhattan; find the place with its search box
- **BBBike MapCompare** — [mc.bbbike.org/mc](https://mc.bbbike.org/mc/) — four basemaps of the same viewport side by side
- **LiDAR Lens (Britain and Ireland)** — [lidarlens.sarsen.org](https://lidarlens.sarsen.org/) — open-government LiDAR terrain models of England, Wales, Scotland and Ireland under a movable relief lens, with historic-environment records (`#map={z}/{lat}/{lng}`). Greyed out when the view is outside its bounds (48.85° to 61.6° N, 16° W to 9° E). Its terrain is fixed when the site is built (one configuration per country, tiles made by its own worker from each agency's service), and nothing in its URL or settings takes another elevation source such as Mapterhorn, so it cannot open elsewhere; it is last in the list for that reason

The zoom-to-camera translation is destination-specific — e.g. the Google Earth/Maps links convert the map zoom into a viewing altitude, and ESRI Wayback opens on its own latest release.

## Custom destinations

**Add custom…** accepts your own URL template with either placeholder family:

- `{lat}` / `{lng}` / `{zoom}` — e.g. `https://example.com/?lat={lat}&lng={lng}&zoom={zoom}`
- slippy-tile `{z}` / `{x}` / `{y}` — e.g. `https://example.com/#{z}/{x}/{y}`

Custom entries appear in the same dropdown and can be removed from it at any time.

Other viewers worth knowing that take no location in their URL, so cannot be a destination here (Aguacero WX, Senter Farms' CutSheetRX), are on [Related Work](/resources/related-work).
