The videos are made the same way as the [slide decks](/features/slide-decks): by an agent, from the real app. An agent drives the production site in a headless Chrome on the GPU, a DevTools screencast captures the frames, and the cut adds title cards and a narrator's subtitles in Remotion. The files live on the [demo-takes release](https://github.com/Iconem/terrain-viewer/releases/tag/demo-takes) and stream from there; the scripts of every take are under [`video/takes/`](https://github.com/Iconem/terrain-viewer/tree/main/video/takes).

## Making them

The recording recipe, the capture over the DevTools protocol, the Remocn components of the cut and the commands are on [Making the demo videos](/dev/demo-videos). The release holds the current cut and the two takes it was made from; superseded recordings are removed when a new one lands.
